OSCE Countdown has no accounts, so there is no account to close and nothing to sign in to. Most app data lives only on your device. The data we may hold is limited to optional messages, launch-notification requests, short-lived abuse-protection counters, crash reports, adverts and purchase records handled by the services named below.

## 1. Data on your device

Your presets, exam configurations and settings live locally on your phone and nowhere else. We never upload them and we cannot see them, so there is nothing to ask us to remove.

- **To delete everything:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage from your phone's settings. That removes every preset, configuration and setting permanently.

- **Individual presets:** delete any preset from the Presets list inside the app.

- **Purchases are safe:** Remove Ads and Exam Circuit belong to your App Store or Google Play account, not to the local app settings. Reinstall and use Restore Purchases to ask the store and RevenueCat for your entitlements again.

## 2. Data we might hold

Optional forms can send us something, and anything we still hold from them is deleted on request. Email support@oscecountdown.app with the subject "Delete my data" and we will remove it. You do not have to tell us why.

- **Feedback you sent us:** the message, the technical line the form showed you before sending, and a two-letter country code. We keep it while we act on it and delete it within 12 months at the outside. Because the form has no account behind it, include some of the message's wording in your request so we can find it.

- **Website contact messages:** your email address, message, and name if you gave one. Include the email address you used and the approximate date of the message so we can find it in the support inbox.

- **Launch notifications:** the email address and platform you submitted before the app was available in the stores. These requests are not a mailing list; email us from the same address and we will remove the message if we still hold it.

Rate-limit counters for feedback requests delete themselves within two hours and are not joined to a message.

## 3. Data held by services the app uses

A few services process data under their own policies, none of it tied to an account with us:

- **Google AdMob** shows occasional adverts in the free version after practice sessions, unless you have Remove Ads. Manage Google ad personalisation at Google's ad settings.

- **RevenueCat** holds an anonymous purchase record so the app can restore what you own on a new device. The app does not give RevenueCat your name or email address.

- **Sentry** receives a technical report if the app crashes or a serious error is caught. The app does not attach an account, name or email address to those reports.

- **Apple and Google** process your purchases under their own terms. We never see your payment details.

## More detail

The full picture of what we handle, why, and for how long is in the Privacy Policy. Anything else, ask us via the contact form and we will handle it under your UK GDPR rights.
