## 2. Information We Collect

There is no requirement for you to provide your name, email address or any
other personal identifying information before you use our app.

### App data stored on your device

Your presets, current configurations, settings, ad-display state, purchase
entitlement cache and legal-document read markers are stored locally on your
device. We do not upload these app settings to our servers and cannot see
them. Deleting the app, or clearing its app storage, deletes them from your
device.

### Feedback you send from the app

If you use Send Feedback in the app, we receive the message you type, the
category you chose, the diagnostic line shown before you send it, and a
two-letter country code supplied by Cloudflare. The diagnostic line includes
basic technical details such as platform, operating system version, app
version, build number and update identifier.

The feedback form does not collect your email address, so we normally cannot
reply unless you include contact details in the message yourself. Feedback is
sent to us by email through Mailgun and is also held in a private database so
we can review and track it. We delete feedback once it is no longer needed,
and in any event within 12 months.

### Website contact form

If you contact us through the website, we receive the information you submit:
your email address, your message, and your name if you provide one. Contact
form messages are sent to us by email through Mailgun so we can read and
respond to your enquiry.

Our website sets no cookies.

### Abuse protection

Our feedback relay uses short-lived counters to limit misuse. It counts
feedback requests per IP address and deletes those counters automatically
after two hours. These counters are not stored with feedback messages.

Website contact and notification forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people
and bots apart. Cloudflare receives technical details of the request, including
your IP address, browser User-Agent header, TLS fingerprint and our site
identifier. We pass your IP address to Cloudflare for the check but do not
keep it in the website form message.

### Adverts

The free version may show a Google AdMob interstitial advert when a practice
session ends, including if you stopped it early. You can optionally decide to
remove Ads by making a one-time in-app purchase.

AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers and ad
interaction data under Google's policies. In the UK and EEA, the app asks for
your consent before requesting adverts; if you refuse, adverts are not
personalised where Google permits non-personalised ads. On iOS, your App
Tracking Transparency choice is respected.

### Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play and managed with
RevenueCat. RevenueCat keeps an anonymous purchase record so purchases can be
restored on a new device. We do not receive your card or payment details.

### Crash and error reports

If the app crashes or a serious error is caught, a technical report may be
sent to Sentry on servers in the European Union. Reports can include device
model, operating system version, app version and error details. We do not
attach an account, name or email address to crash reports, and the app is
configured not to send default personal identifiers.

## 4. Lawful Basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on:

- consent for personalised advertising;
- contract where processing is needed to provide app purchases and restore
  entitlements;
- legitimate interests for answering messages, operating support channels,
  protecting against abuse, understanding crashes and maintaining the app; and
- legal obligation where we must keep or disclose information to comply with
  the law.

You can withdraw consent at any time. You can also object to processing based
on legitimate interests.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you,
restrict or object to its use, or withdraw consent. Contact us at
oscecountdown.app/contact or support@oscecountdown.app. We will respond

Because OSCE Countdown has no accounts and most app data stays on your device,
some requests need a practical step from you. The guide at

ask us to remove anything we may hold.
